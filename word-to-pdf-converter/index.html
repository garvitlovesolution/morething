About the Word to PDF Converter

Upload a Word (.docx) file and get a PDF in a few seconds. The file is converted inside your browser and never uploaded. Simple documents with text, headings, lists, tables and images convert well.

How to use it

Choose a .docx file and press Convert to PDF. When the file is ready, click Download PDF.

Common questions

Will the PDF look exactly like my Word file?

Simple documents convert well. Complex layouts, special fonts or text boxes can change a little, because the conversion happens in your browser.

Are my files or text uploaded?

No. This tool runs in your browser, so what you enter is not sent to our servers.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free to use with no sign-up.
