About the Compound Interest Calculator

See how an amount grows when interest is added back and earns interest too. Useful for fixed deposits and savings plans.

How to use it

Enter the amount, the yearly rate, the years and how often interest is added. You see the maturity amount and the interest earned.

Common questions

Why does compounding frequency matter?

More frequent compounding adds interest to the balance sooner, so the final amount is slightly higher.

Are my files or text uploaded?

No. This tool runs in your browser, so what you enter is not sent to our servers.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free to use with no sign-up.
