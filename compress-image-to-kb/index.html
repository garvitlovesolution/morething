About the Compress Image to KB

Upload a photo and enter the size you need, like 50 KB or 200 KB for a form. The tool finds the best quality that fits and gives you a JPG.

How to use it

Choose a photo, enter the size you need in KB and wait a moment. Download the compressed JPG when it appears.

Common questions

Why can the tool miss my target size?

Very small targets on large photos are hard to reach. Shrink the image with the Image Resizer first, then compress it.

Are my files or text uploaded?

No. This tool runs in your browser, so what you enter is not sent to our servers.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free to use with no sign-up.
