# Code of Conduct

We want MoreThing to be a welcoming place for everyone.

**We expect:** respect for different people and views, helpful and constructive feedback, and a focus on what is best for the project.

**We do not accept:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, or sharing private information without permission.

Maintainers may remove comments or contributions that break these rules and may ban people who repeat them. To report a problem, email velvetloom2@gmail.com.
