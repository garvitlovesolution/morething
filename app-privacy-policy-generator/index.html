About the App Privacy Policy Generator

Fill in your app details and get a plain-language privacy policy to start from. Google Play and the App Store ask for one. This is a template, not legal advice, so check it fits what your app really does.

How to use it

Enter your app name, your name and email, answer two questions and copy the policy text.

Common questions

Is the policy legally enough?

It is a starting template only. Apps with accounts, payments or children as users need a lawyer to review it.

Are my files or text uploaded?

No. This tool runs in your browser, so what you enter is not sent to our servers.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free to use with no sign-up.
