About Autopay Guard

List every subscription and auto-debit, see what leaves your account each month and get a calendar warning before each charge.

How to use it

Enter the name, the monthly amount and the day of the month it is charged, then press Save autopay. Press Warn me before each charge to add monthly reminders, two days and one day before, to your phone calendar. Tap Still needed to mark an item as checked.

Common questions

How does it warn me?

It creates a calendar file with monthly reminders two days and one day before each charge. Your calendar app does the reminding, even when this site is closed.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You type the details yourself, and nothing is sent anywhere. That is why it is private.
