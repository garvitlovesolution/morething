About Money Ledger

A private money notebook: add spending and income, see this month at a glance and download a CSV.

How to use it

Choose Spent or Received, enter the amount and a category, and press Save entry. The panel shows what is left this month and where money went. Press Download CSV to open it in a spreadsheet.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only in this browser on this device. There is no account and nothing is sent to our servers.

Can I move it to another phone?

Use Download backup on the homepage, then Restore on the other device.
