About funding.json Generator

Create a funding.json file for FLOSS/fund and other open source funders, with a checklist of anything missing.

How to use it

Fill in who you are, your project, its repository and license, how people can pay you and the yearly amount you ask for. Press Create funding.json, fix anything in the checklist, then download the file and publish it on your website or repository.

Common questions

Where do I put the file?

Publish it at the root of your website, like https://yoursite.com/funding.json, or in your repository. Then submit its address to the funder.

Is my information uploaded?

No. Everything is created in your browser and nothing is sent to our servers.
